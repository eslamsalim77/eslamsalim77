## About

Flutter developer with 5+ years of experience taking mobile products from architecture to App Store and Google Play release. I currently work remotely at **SkyCrew**, leading development of the Aabir ride-hailing apps for Qatar, and I build products for clients in Saudi Arabia and Egypt.

- **Clean Architecture** — layered, testable codebases (MVVM, SOLID, dependency injection).
- **Real-time systems** — live tracking, WebSockets, video streaming and background audio.
- **Store-ready delivery** — CI/CD and releases on Google Play and the App Store.
- **Remote collaboration** — comfortable working with distributed, international teams.
